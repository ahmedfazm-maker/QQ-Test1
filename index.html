Business profile

Reconstructed from evidence, not from the deck
Meshwar sells a fleet dispatch and proof-of-delivery platform to FMCG distributors in Greater Cairo and Alexandria. Revenue is a per-vehicle monthly subscription (EGP 340–520) plus a one-off onboarding fee, billed quarterly in advance.
Nineteen paying distributors across 1,140 tracked vehicles. Recognised revenue of EGP 5.9M in FY24, growing 31% year on year. Eleven staff on payroll; two further contributors invoice as contractors. Incorporated in Egypt as a single LLC, no holding structure.
Positioning in the deck emphasises AI route optimisation. The product analytics show route optimisation used by 3 of 19 accounts; proof-of-delivery is used daily by all 19. The business being bought is not the business being pitched.
